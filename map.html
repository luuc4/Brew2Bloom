The Map

Drop. Pick up. Repeat.

Eight Vorarlberg locations, two kinds of pin. Brown drops are partner cafés where you can leave your own grounds; green pickups are Bloom Hubs where kits and refills wait.

Drop your grounds (café partner) Pick up your kit (Bloom Hub)

    Saves 50 kg of grounds every month.

    Marktstraße 12 · Dornbirn

  • Bloom Hub Bregenz Pickup

    Pick up your kit & substrate refills.

    Kaiserstraße 28 · Bregenz

  • Drip & Co Drop

    Daily 3 kg of premium organic grounds.

    Neustadt 14 · Feldkirch

  • The Loop Drop

    Customers love picking up kits at the counter.

    Rathausstraße 6 · Bregenz

  • Bloom Hub Dornbirn Pickup

    Workshops every Saturday at 14:00.

    Schulgasse 9 · Dornbirn

  • Espresso Park Drop

    Maria-Theresien-Straße 3 · Lustenau

    Bahnhofstraße 22 · Hohenems

  • Bloom Hub Bludenz Pickup

    Community garden out back, open Sundays.

    Mutterstraße 4 · Bludenz

Our four intentions

Why we do this.

The pillars under every kit, every workshop, every café partnership.

Resource Conservation

Divert tons of valuable coffee grounds from the landfill, reintegrate them into the nutrient cycle as high-quality organic substrate.

Promoting Urban Gardening

Empower city dwellers to grow their own vegetables — even in limited spaces. Simple, affordable, sustainable.

Education & Awareness

Show how a circular economy works on a small scale — right on your balcony or in your kitchen.

Local Networks

Bridge local cafés and the neighborhoods around them. Build a community dedicated to urban resilience.

Why cucumbers love coffee.

Acidity, nitrogen, drainage. Three reasons your vines will thrive on yesterday's espresso — and one common mistake to avoid.

Spent coffee grounds are nearly pH-neutral once brewed (around 6.5), which is exactly what cucurbits prefer. They release nitrogen slowly, improve cation exchange, and aerate compacted balcony soil. Done right, they replace half of your standard potting mix — done wrong, they compact and starve roots of oxygen…

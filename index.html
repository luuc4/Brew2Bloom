Mission

A circular economy you can taste.

Four intentions guide everything we do — from the kit on your shelf to the workshops in your neighborhood.

Resource Conservation

We divert tons of valuable coffee grounds from the landfill and reintegrate them into the nutrient cycle as high-quality organic substrate.

Promoting Urban Gardening

We empower city dwellers to grow their own vegetables — cucumbers, salads, herbs — even in a shoebox apartment. Simple, affordable, sustainable.

Education & Awareness

We give you the tools and knowledge to see how circular economy works on a small scale — right on your balcony or in your kitchen.

Local Networks

We bridge local cafés and the neighborhoods around them, fostering a community dedicated to sustainability and urban resilience.

The Loop

From espresso shot to dinner plate.

Four small steps, one big circle. No landfill in sight.

01 / Brew

Brew

Your local café collects spent grounds in our Brew2Bloom bins instead of throwing them out.

02 / Bloom

Bloom

We pH-balance the grounds, mix in biochar and pack them with organic seeds into your kit.

03 / Harvest

Harvest

Cucumbers, salads, herbs and chilis — grown in your kitchen, watered with rainwater, eaten the same week.

04 / Repeat

Repeat

Subscribe and we deliver fresh substrate every season. Compost the old, grow the new. The loop continues.
